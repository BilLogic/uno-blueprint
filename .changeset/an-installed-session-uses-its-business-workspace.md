An installed Uno session reads its linked project and writes its findings ledger in the caller's business workspace, with an optional `--workspace` override. Unknown profiles produce a sentence refusal, Init records identify their source, and the approval audit record uses the cell-edit payload with the prior approval pair inside its inverse. New cell columns need explicit UPDATE grants; the Cursor guide remains unverified.

**Upgrading:** apply the new migration with `supabase db push` to update approval audit payloads. Existing authoring records stay unchanged.
