Dragging a lane or step beyond its allowed section now allows a small, progressively resisted rubber-band pull past its boundary, including the board’s outer edges. The limited overshoot returns smoothly on an invalid release without placing the item in another section. Repeated pulls spring back from their actual released position. Reduced motion returns immediately, and an out-of-bounds drop never saves.

Side-panel order rails keep dragged items inside their list, clear of boundary labels. Step rails show Beginning and End; lane rails retain their section boundaries.
