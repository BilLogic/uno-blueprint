The composer's Send button sits inside the field. It is anchored to the field's bottom-right corner, so it stays beside the last line as a draft grows, and the field's text wraps before it in both the field and the coloured copy behind it. While a turn runs, Stop takes the same slot instead of standing outside the field. Keyboard behaviour, disabled states, tooltips and labels are unchanged, and an empty draft no longer dims the whole field just because Send is disabled. Nothing to do when upgrading a deployment.
